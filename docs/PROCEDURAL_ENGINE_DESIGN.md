## 1. Engineering Disclaimer & Geometry Classification

To maintain scientific integrity and avoid misleading claims, the 3D model generated by AERIS is explicitly classified as:

> **Classification: Visually Plausible Procedural Aerospace Geometry (Category 1)**  
> This model is an interactive architectural representation of a high-bypass dual-spool turbofan engine designed for spatial human-computer interaction and real-time visualization. It is **not** dimensionally certified CAD geometry (Category 2), nor is it a physically validated aerothermal simulation model (Category 3). All dimensions, blade counts, and compression ratios are parametric visual approximations designed for educational and interaction fidelity.

## 3. Sub-Assembly Engineering Specifications

### 3.1 Stage 1: Intake Spinner & Fan Blisk
- **Visual Purpose:** Frontmost visible rotating assembly; demonstrates wide-chord aerodynamic sweep and intake aerodynamics.
- **Geometry Generation:**
  - *Spinner:* `THREE.ConeGeometry` or `THREE.LatheGeometry` with elliptical parabolic profile curve ($r(z) = R \sqrt{1 - z/L}$).
  - *Fan Blades:* 24 instanced airfoil meshes generated via `THREE.ExtrudeGeometry` along a twisted cubic Bezier path to produce true aerodynamic blade twist from root to tip.
- **Spool Coupling:** Attached to the inner LP shaft.
- **Exploded Transform:** Displaces along $+Z$ axis (forward) by $+1.8$ units.
- **Material Assignment:** Anodized titanium alloy with brushed radial specular highlight.

### 3.2 Stage 2: Low-Pressure Compressor (LPC / Booster)
- **Visual Purpose:** Compresses bypass air entering the core; illustrates multi-stage rotor disks.
- **Geometry Generation:**
  - 3 stepped rotor disks (`THREE.CylinderGeometry`) with 36 micro-blades per disk generated via `THREE.InstancedMesh`.
  - Stationary stator vane rings interleaved between disks.
- **Spool Coupling:** Attached to LP shaft.
- **Exploded Transform:** Displaces forward along $+Z$ axis by $+1.1$ units.

### 3.3 Stage 3: High-Pressure Compressor (HPC Core)
- **Visual Purpose:** High-density axial compression core; illustrates steep tapering and increasing compression ratios.
- **Geometry Generation:**
  - 6 progressively smaller tapered drum stages (`THREE.CylinderGeometry` with decreasing radius $R_1 \to R_6$).
  - 6 rings of high-density axial blades (48 to 72 blades per ring) managed via a single `THREE.InstancedMesh` with dynamic transform matrices.
- **Spool Coupling:** Attached to outer HP shaft (rotates at $4\times$ the visual speed of the LP spool).
- **Exploded Transform:** Displaces forward along $+Z$ by $+0.5$ units.

### 3.4 Stage 4: Annular Combustor
- **Visual Purpose:** Hot section combustion chamber; demonstrates flame tube cooling holes and fuel atomization nozzles.
- **Geometry Generation:**
  - Double-walled annular ring (`THREE.RingGeometry` / `THREE.CylinderGeometry` with inner and outer liner wall).
  - 18 circular fuel nozzle injector assemblies arranged circumferentially ($2\pi / 18$ spacing).
  - Parametric cooling hole texture or alpha-tested grid shader.
- **Spool Coupling:** Static (attached to core engine frame).
- **Exploded Transform:** Remains near engine center ($Z = 0.0$ units) as reference anchor.
- **Material Assignment:** Thermal barrier ceramic coating (matte off-white/ochre) with heat-tinted Inconel weld seams.

### 3.5 Stage 5: High-Pressure Turbine (HPT)
- **Visual Purpose:** Extracts energy from combustor gas to drive the HPC; shows single-crystal cooled turbine blades.
- **Geometry Generation:**
  - Heavy forged rotor disc with peripheral dovetail slots.
  - 54 high-temperature turbine blades with internal cooling passage indicators.
- **Spool Coupling:** Rigidly attached to outer HP shaft.
- **Exploded Transform:** Displaces aft along $-Z$ axis by $-0.6$ units.

### 3.6 Stage 6: Low-Pressure Turbine (LPT)
- **Visual Purpose:** Multi-stage low-temperature extraction turbine that absorbs remaining gas energy to drive the main fan.
- **Geometry Generation:**
  - 3 expanding-diameter rotor disks with high aspect-ratio turbine blades.
- **Spool Coupling:** Attached to central LP shaft.
- **Exploded Transform:** Displaces aft along $-Z$ axis by $-1.2$ units.

### 3.7 Stage 7: Exhaust Nozzle & Centerbody Plug
- **Visual Purpose:** Aerodynamic gas discharge; shows centerbody plug cone and mixer lobes.
- **Geometry Generation:**
  - Aerodynamic centerbody bullet cone (`THREE.LatheGeometry`).
  - Convergent outer discharge duct.
- **Spool Coupling:** Static.
- **Exploded Transform:** Displaces aft along $-Z$ axis by $-1.9$ units.

### 3.8 Stage 8: Structural Bypass Nacelle & Casing
- **Visual Purpose:** Engine enclosure; cutaway casing reveals internal rotating spools.
- **Geometry Generation:**
  - Cylindrical nacelle with a $120^\circ$ radial cutaway opening (`THREE.CylinderGeometry` with `thetaLength = Math.PI * 1.33`).
- **Exploded Transform:** Radially expands outwards along $+Y$ and $+X$ axes by $+1.5$ units.
- **Material Assignment:** Dual-sided brushed composite casing with semi-transparent tinted glass or CAD wireframe overlay.

## 4. Draw-Call Optimization via `THREE.InstancedMesh`

A naive implementation of a jet engine with hundreds of individual turbine blades would create over 400 separate draw calls, bottlenecking the WebGL pipeline.
AERIS optimizes geometry using **Instanced Mesh Clustering**:

1. **Shared Blade Geometries:** A single base blade geometry is created for the fan, one for the compressor, and one for the turbine.
2. **Matrix Transformations:** Each blade instance in a ring is positioned via a pre-calculated 4x4 transform matrix:
   $$\mathbf{M}_i = \mathbf{T}(0, 0, z_{\text{stage}}) \times \mathbf{R}_z\left(\frac{2\pi i}{N}\right) \times \mathbf{R}_y(\text{twist}) \times \mathbf{S}(s_x, s_y, s_z)$$
3. **Result:** The entire 6-stage HPC blisk assembly (>350 blades) is rendered in **exactly 1 draw call** instead of 350.
4. **Draw Call Budget:** The entire complete procedural engine renders in **fewer than 20 draw calls**, guaranteeing consistent 60 FPS performance even on low-end integrated GPUs.

## 5. Dual Visual Rendering Modes

AERIS supports two switchable visual rendering modes operating over the identical component geometry:

### Mode A: Technical CAD / Hologram Mode (Default — Reference Aesthetic)
- **Concept:** Wireframe structural lines, dark obsidian surfaces, and high-contrast glowing amber/cyan telemetry vectors (matching user reference imagery).
- **Shader Pipeline:** Custom `THREE.ShaderMaterial` with edge detection and Fresnel rim lighting. Emphasizes mechanical structural silhouettes and blade angles without opaque occlusion.
- **Highlight Effect:** Hovered/selected components intensify emissive bloom ($I_{\text{emissive}} \times 3.0$).

### Mode B: Photorealistic Aerospace PBR Mode
- **Concept:** Physically Based Rendering (PBR) using `THREE.MeshStandardMaterial` / `MeshPhysicalMaterial`.
- **Properties:**
  - Fan/Compressor: Metallic $= 0.85$, Roughness $= 0.25$ (Machined Titanium Ti-6Al-4V).
  - Combustor: Metallic $= 0.1$, Roughness $= 0.8$ (Ceramic Thermal Barrier Coating YSZ).
  - Turbine: Metallic $= 0.9$, Roughness $= 0.35$ with subtle rainbow iridescence (High-temperature oxidized Inconel 718).
